## Incident: c1 killed by our own h5 cleaner (fixed)

- 137985 ran iterates 0–6 healthily (t_pk 0.9043 → 0.9199, W 18.368 → 18.524,
  ≈ 0.106 T/µm — the historic uniform-seed exchange rate), was PREEMPTED
  mid-it7, then two resume incarnations died identically:
  `Can not find result 'E' in field_profile_adj` ~49 min into gradient
  assembly.
- Root cause (confirmed by the fwd_default/ dir mtime coinciding with a */10
  cron firing): `h5_clean_once.sh` pass 1 kept only the 2 newest
  `*_output.h5` older than 30 min per study dir. A slow resumed gradient
  (40 min adjoint + 49 min assembly on the requeue node) leaves the FORWARD
  h5 >30 min old and ranked 3rd behind the port- and width-adjoint outputs →
  deleted mid-gradient → the assembly's re-read of forward monitor values
  crashes. Fast 66-min iterates never crossed the threshold; IGUM (no
  cleaner) survived a 16.8 h gradient.
- Fix (deployed to `~/h5_clean_once.sh` on Athena + this repo's
  `athena/h5_clean_once.sh`): age floor 30 min → **4 h**, keep-count 2 → **4**
  (forward + two adjoints + margin). Rule captured: a cleaner's age floor
  must exceed the longest live need-window; keep-count must exceed the
  live-file count.
- c1 resubmitted as **job 138535** (4d_1g / 96 h / 256G, same label = resume,
  loss ≤ 1 eval). Started healthy on n310, GPU confirmed.

## Measured conclusion: climb-phase dw_pred is dominated by slaving, and the
## direct term overpredicts narrowing

c1 climb rows predict dw_pred < 0 (−0.001 … −0.081) while measured ΔW > 0
(+0.011 … +0.041). Decomposition: measured ΔW ≈ 0.3655 × measured Δλ to
~±25% every iterate — the λ-slaving chain term explains essentially ALL real
width motion, while the fixed-λ direct term's predicted narrowing does not
materialize. b1 it0 similarly underpredicted (+0.030 pred vs +0.152 meas;
it1 fine: +0.107 vs +0.120). Implications: (a) climb/filter logic is
unaffected (accepts on measured values); (b) ride-leak expectations should
be set by the chain term's fidelity, not the direct term's; (c) the online
dW/dλ refit (b1) and the λ-drift step bound remain the right guards.

## MEASURED (night): fast-then-rescale test verdict — the THIRD outcome

Task 49 (job 138575, canary at pitch 516.427 = f·516.83): the s5 fast best
rescaled lands at **T 0.93656 / λ 1564.778 / FWHM 18.8057 µm** (stored
drifted point: 0.93604 / 1565.835 / 18.827; uniform seed: 0.9012 / 1564.614
/ 18.345). So:
- T fully survives the rescale (+0.0005) and λ recovers 87% (pitch-retune
  efficiency 0.87 — dispersion + fixed height).
- **The width does NOT come back** (−0.021 µm, = the trivial ×f scaling; the
  slaving prediction of −0.45 was wrong for THIS direction). λ and W are
  SEPARABLE: pitch moves λ at ~fixed W; parameter moves along gradient paths
  move them together.
- Interpretation: the fast campaign's T gain is ATTACHED TO THE WIDTH, not
  the wavelength — "run fast + rescale" yields T-at-fixed-λ but an
  out-of-band mode (18.81 = +2.5%). Since the spec binds W, the projected
  (width-held) approach is JUSTIFIED; the fast route cannot deliver an
  in-band design without paying the width back at the measured ~0.11 T/µm.
- Bonus: this resolves the old open question — T CAN rise at fixed λ; it
  cannot rise (this far) at fixed W. Also: a global-scale 192nd parameter
  (lit item 3) is a FREE λ-recentring lever (T-neutral, W-neutral), which
  can replace per-step λ-drift anxiety in future campaigns.
