## Physics interpretation and remaining limits

Qc=QL/sqrt(T) and Qi=QL/(1-sqrt(T)) assume the symmetric isolated two-port resonance model. Background/Fano response, normalization errors, asymmetry and nonconverged spectra can invalidate the inferred loss split. At high T, Qi is ill-conditioned: relative sensitivity to relative T error is sqrt(T)/(2(1-sqrt(T))). A single row fixes levels, not coupling growth, loss saturation or asymptotic width independently.

CMT remains useful for this separate predictor. It must not become the TE optimizer's width wall or revive sigma as a FWHM proxy. The TE corrugation transformation borrows TM exponents; its uncertainty is not independently calibrated. Sparse families and corrugation corrections informed by earlier misses need prospective validation; the current checks are not a universal confidence interval. The quoted spectral noise weights are historical family assumptions, not newly established numerical noise bounds.
