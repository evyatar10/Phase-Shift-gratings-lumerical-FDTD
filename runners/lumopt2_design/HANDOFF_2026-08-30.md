## Measured facts a re-think must respect (all cited in CHANGES/LIT files)

1. **λ-chain gradient fix works**: c1's climb ΔW ≈ 0.3655 × Δλ measured
   every iterate; dlam predictions land within ~25%; the ride toy cut width
   growth 75-80%. A Nov-2025 paper (arXiv:2511.16643) independently shows
   this chain term is the conditioning fix for resonance-tracked objectives
   (O(Q²) ill-conditioning without it).
2. **Pitch rescale separates λ from W** (task 49, job 138575): rescaling the
   fast unconstrained best (t_pk 0.93604, λ +1.221 nm drift, W 18.827) by
   f=0.99922 gives t_pk 0.93656 / λ 1564.778 / W 18.806 — T survives, λ
   returns (87%), W does NOT return. So: T can rise at fixed λ; the width is
   the binding currency; a final pitch trim onto the target λ is free.
3. **Exchange rates**: uniform-seed lane ≈ 0.106 T/µm of width; fast
   unconstrained lane 0.072 T/µm; b1's climb from BEST 0.0036 T/µm.
   Shadow price gT·gW alignment: ~0.001-0.005 along c1's path, 0.062 at
   BEST (⇒ projection forfeits ~6% of ∇T there — interpretation, not
   measurement, that b1 is step-limited rather than saturated; the user
   was not convinced).
4. **T vs loss bookkeeping**: c1's t_pk matches (1 − Q_L/Q_i)² to 4 digits
   (Q_L 1951, Q_i 47500 → 0.9195). (This session's framing of "T gains =
   loss engineering" is interpretation on top of that identity.)
5. **Pace context from literature**: published adjoint cavity optimizations
   run 100-1000+ iterations; ~30 is early. (The user finds the absolute
   wall-clock unacceptable regardless — a re-think should treat wall-clock
   per unit ΔT, not iteration count, as the metric.)
6. **σ vs FWHM**: σ is tail-weighted and blind to apodization reshaping
   (historic +14.9% FWHM at ~flat σ); drift dilates both. The FWHM spec is
   physical (acoustic overlap), σ is only a logged cross-check.
7. **No published precedent** for an envelope-FWHM-constrained cavity
   design was found; mode-volume constraints are the nearest neighbours
   (Işıklar 2022). Candidate method upgrades from the review, unimplemented:
